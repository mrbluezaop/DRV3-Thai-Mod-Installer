Format references
-----------------
The installer contains an independently written parser/decompressor informed
by public documentation and open-source implementations of CRI CPK, UTF and
CRILAYLA formats, including:

- cpktools by kamikat (MIT License)
  https://github.com/kamikat/cpktools
- CriPakTools by esperknight
  https://github.com/esperknight/CriPakTools
- Harmony-Tools wiki by redssu
  https://github.com/redssu/Harmony-Tools/wiki/Danganronpa-V3%27s-file-types-and-structure

No third-party executable, CpkMaker.dll, YACpkTool, or original game archive
is included in this package.
